ABOUT

Digital Fortune Cookie is a playful web-based project that generates fortunes using random quotes from movies, books, and music. By pulling fragments from familiar media, the site turns cultural references into unexpected messages, creating moments of surprise and connection. Rather than offering traditional advice, each fortune invites users to interpret meaning through their own memories and associations. The project explores how randomness, media, and interaction can transform everyday quotes into a lighthearted but engaging experience.
