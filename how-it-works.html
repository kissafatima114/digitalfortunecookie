HOW IT WORKS

When you crack the digital fortune cookie, the website randomly selects a quote from movies, books, or music. Each quote functions as a fortune, but its meaning is not fixed or prescriptive.

Instead, the interpretation is left entirely up to the person receiving it. Like a traditional fortune cookie, the message only gains significance if the reader chooses to believe in it, reflect on it, or manifest it in their own way.

This project plays with the idea that meaning is created through personal association rather than certainty, turning randomness into intention.
